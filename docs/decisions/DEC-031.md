# DEC-031: Dependabot PRs #38–#46 closed as superseded by the universal lock and the Phase 1 toolchain bump (ev

## Context

Recorded as a governance decision on the historical pipe log.

## Decision

Dependabot PRs #38-#46 are closed as superseded (tech-debt-hardening-plan R-TDH-10 / AC-10). Each proposed a single-package bump against the pre-lock layout; the universal `requirements-lock.txt` and the Phase 1 toolchain bump carry every one of them at or above the proposed version: #38 pytest-mock 3.15.1, #39 ruff 0.16.4 (branch: 0.16.5), #42 tomli 2.4.1, #43 knip 6.32.3, #44 eslint 10.9.1, #45 typescript-eslint 8.68.0, #46 @types/node 26.4.0, #41 pydantic >=2.13.5 (requirements.txt already declares it). #40 is closed for a different reason and not adopted: raising the fastapi floor to >=0.141.1 would break the Python 3.9 leg, because fastapi 0.141 requires 3.10; the lock resolves 0.141.1 on 3.10+ and 0.128.8 on 3.9, which is the correct shape until the floor moves (DEC-028). Merging any of the nine on top of the lock would either be a no-op or reintroduce a per-package pin the lock supersedes. Closing rather than leaving them open keeps the queue honest: an open bot PR reads as pending work. Dependabot proposes the next releases against the new baseline once #61 merges; the weekly `lock-upgrade-check` job (R-TDH-9) is the Python-side signal from then on
