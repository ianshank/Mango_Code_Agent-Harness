# DEC-004: Unwired policy keys are classified with reviewed reasons instead of deleted

## Context

Recorded as a governance decision on the historical pipe log.

## Decision

Unwired governance-policy keys are classified (DECLARED_NOT_YET_ENFORCED in test_policy_consistency.py) rather than deleted -- per-stack mirrors sit under root-of-trust and bundle digests, so removal is a rotation-sized change; the duplicated decision-ID grammar, agent_defaults limits, and gitleaks pin become cross-checked equalities; the stale unread harness/SHA256SUMS.txt manifest is deleted; Python coverage is enforced as two separate floors (lines and branches) by coverage_gate.py; build_policy_bundle.py joins digest-regen as the only regenerator of the bundle's top-level policy digests
