# DEC-034: `make lint-node` is wired into `make ci`, and the recorded blocker was not the real one

## Context

Recorded as a governance decision on the historical pipe log.

## Decision

`make lint-node` is wired into `make ci`, and the blocker on record was not the real one. DEC-013 and `docs/specs/ci-enforcement-gaps.md` recorded a `typescript` 7.0.2 / `typescript-eslint` 8.67.0 incompatibility. Measured against the installed workspace on 2026-09-03 with the current pins (`typescript` ~6.0.3, `typescript-eslint` 8.68.0): ESLint passes, Knip passes, and the failure is `prettier --check` on exactly one file, `harness/node/.governance/policy.json`, over whitespace inside the `coverage.optional_extras.path_prefixes` array DEC-028 added. That file is digest-pinned -- `docs/rca/e2e_origin_sync_triage_rca_v2.5.0.md` records Prettier's reformat breaking the root-of-trust SHA256 and the remediation being to restore the exact bytes -- so Prettier's preferred formatting and `validate_adoption.py` are mutually exclusive by construction and running `prettier --write` is what breaks the digest, not what fixes the lint. Resolved by adding `harness/node/.prettierignore` excluding the digest-pinned `.governance/` tree, which is the only move that lets both gates hold; `make lint-node` then exits 0 and `make validate` still reports `adoption: passed`. `lint-node` becomes a direct prerequisite of `ci` and deliberately not of `ci-python` or the shared `lint` target, because the secondary matrix legs install no pnpm. This brings R-TDH-23's policy-sourced ESLint `max-lines` rule into a CI job for the first time -- it was enforced nowhere. Considered and rejected: reformatting `policy.json` and re-pinning the digest (it would recur on every DEC-028-shaped policy edit, and re-pinning a root-of-trust digest to satisfy a formatter inverts which artefact is authoritative)
