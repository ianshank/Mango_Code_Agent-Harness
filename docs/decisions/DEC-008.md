# DEC-008: Role tool exposure is derived from `agent-policy.json` (union of canonical contracts minus approval-

## Context

Recorded as a governance decision on the historical pipe log.

## Decision

Tool exposure is derived per active role from agent-policy.json rather than defaulting to the full schema. The union of a role's canonical contracts minus each contract's human_approval_required_for is the effective grant, so release-auditor's approval-gated external_write and production_change do not leak into the verifier

## Consequences

the verifier no longer receives write_file, which every canonical contract it maps to already denied in prose. The hook environment is filtered of credential-named variables, enforcing agent-policy.json's declared secrets_may_not_be_propagated_to_subagents
